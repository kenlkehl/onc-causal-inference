Strength distributions

Coefficients follow Normal(mean, SD²); SD = 0 fixes the coefficient. The mean fields and main strength inputs update each other. Binary prevalences and category probabilities follow the distributions chosen in Advanced.

OR below 1 represents a negative logOR. A modifier OR of 1 (or RD of 0) removes that variable from modifier truth. “Both roles” contributes to all three coefficient families.

Advanced inputs · population and simulation

Continuous variables follow Uniform(−√3, √3), with SD 1. All variables are independent in this version, including binary and categorical variables. Category contrasts progress evenly from zero to each realized coefficient. The selector uses coordinate lasso, 500 multiplier draws, and threshold |β| > 10⁻⁷; any selected coordinate retains its group. All encoded candidates enter nuisance fitting. Intercepts are calibrated once on 30,000 separate covariate draws.

How do I establish a recommended sample size?
  1. Pilot: check the mechanism and fitting workflow.
  2. Planning grid: in Advanced, choose “Planning grid,” specify sample sizes, and use at least 200 repetitions per size. Freeze the assumptions, selection rule and success goal first.
  3. Independent confirmation: test the provisional size using fresh simulation seeds and a prespecified number of repetitions. Its one-sided 95% lower probability bound must meet your target.

The current app implements steps 1–2. Confirmation is not yet implemented, so a grid result is a provisional candidate, not a final recommendation. It applies to your assumed population and effects. The grid uses simultaneous lower bounds across evaluated sizes; at 200 repetitions, precision may still be insufficient near your target. No grid starts automatically.

The pilot checks the workflow. It does not establish a recommended sample size.
