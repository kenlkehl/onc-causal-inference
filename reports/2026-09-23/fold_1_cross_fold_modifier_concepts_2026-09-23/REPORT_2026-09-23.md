4. **LLM interpretation**

The most robust modifiers identified across training folds relate to systemic inflammation (NLR), renal function, genomic markers (KRAS G12C, MET), and specific pulmonary symptoms (dyspnea, nodules). Prior treatment history, specifically platinum exposure and surgical lymph node dissection, also surfaces as a strong modifier. Most other physiological and demographic markers lacked consistency across folds.
