# Outer fold 1: multi-model Stage 2 selection

1. Reuse the 352 candidate definitions and complete, type-preserving Gemma 4
   26B A4B extractions already frozen for 800 training and 200 held-out patients.
   Preserve all five original inner folds. Do not rerun extraction or Stage 1.
2. Fit the seven new evidence families using the implemented default policy:
   three repetitions per inner fold; all training rows once, two 80% samples;
   all candidates once per forest family, then random groups of at most 32.
   This produces 305 family/subset cells, including 115 causal forests and 230
   treatment/outcome predictive forests. Nuisance models remain elastic nets.
3. Use Gemma 4 31B at the user-authorized server for aggregate theme review,
   hierarchical reconciliation, and evidence-cited role assignment. Every
   candidate receives a decision. No patient rows, outer-test outcomes, oracle
   labels, or generation metadata enter the role prompts.
4. Freeze selection before fitting the existing production estimator. Fit three
   seeds with selected confounders supplying nuisance adjustment and modifiers
   supplying forest X; retain the historical honest 200-tree forest, minimum
   leaf size 10, sqrt split search, inference enabled, and propensity eligibility
   from 0.1 to 0.9. Report each seed's actual eligible population.
5. Also fit three selection-only comparisons that keep the previous all-candidate
   elastic-net residuals and exact 720/180 overlap population fixed. Only the
   modifier subset changes. This separates the selection comparison from changes
   in nuisance adjustment and propensity eligibility.
6. Freeze all predictions before opening oracle evaluation data. Then audit
   direct confounder/modifier recovery separately from proxy retention; evaluate
   ITE correlation, RMSE, mean bias, variability, and available coverage. Compare
   the matched-population results with saved earlier methods.
7. This is an exploratory rerun after earlier fold-1 oracle diagnostics were seen.
   The algorithm and settings are fixed before new evaluation. It is not an
   untouched confirmatory validation or a five-fold production benchmark.

The scripts and hashes in this directory allow checkpointed resumption. Earlier
experiments and their extraction artifacts are read-only inputs.
