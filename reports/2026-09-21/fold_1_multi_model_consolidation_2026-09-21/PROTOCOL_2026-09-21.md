# Fold 1: global consolidation before oracle evaluation

1. **Reason for the additional pass**
   1. The first multi-model review selected 270 candidates, including 224 modifiers. The user requested consolidation before proceeding with that large feature set.
   2. Preserve the original numerical evidence and role decisions. Stop the expensive broad production refit before any oracle evaluation. Retain a matched-residual 224-modifier forest as the comparison baseline.
2. **Global review**
   1. Gemma 4 31B sees all 352 candidates together, with compact summaries of all seven model families, fold support, effect sizes, uncertainty measures where available, and training measurement availability.
   2. Form explicit groups of redundant measurements. Select actual existing extracted columns; do not merge values, invent measurements, or treat related biology as measurement equivalence.
   3. Select confounder representatives separately. Require each selected confounder to cite its own treatment and outcome evidence. Do not discard adjustment solely because a variable lacks modifier evidence.
   4. Rank at most 32 nonredundant modifier candidates, each citing its own effect evidence and explaining its advantage over alternatives. The cap is an explicit engineering constraint motivated by the broad first pass and the existing 32-feature subset design; it is not inferred from oracle variable counts.
   5. Positive importance or loss differences alone do not establish reliable signal. Require attention to magnitude, negative mean scores, overlapping repetitions, and contradictory methods. Review all candidates, including previously excluded ones.
3. **Choose a compact size within training data**
   1. Compare ranked prefixes of 8, 16, and 32 variables (plus the full shortlist when shorter), and a constant-effect reference, using the five saved inner folds and their already-frozen nested elastic-net residuals.
   2. Preserve the historical 200-tree, honest, inference-enabled causal forest, 45% subsampling, minimum leaf size 10, and sqrt split search. Use two prespecified forest seeds per inner fold and fixed propensity eligibility of 0.1–0.9.
   3. Average seed-specific R-loss within each fold. Find the compact option with the lowest mean fold R-loss, then select the smallest option whose paired excess loss relative to that best option is no more than one estimated standard error across folds. Include the constant option; do not force heterogeneous effects when the evidence is weak.
   4. Also evaluate the broad 224-modifier set as a reference, outside the compact size-selection options. Overlapping folds and prior use of these folds to rank candidates mean this is an adaptive tuning heuristic, not an unbiased performance estimate or formal error-control guarantee.
4. **Final estimation and evaluation**
   1. Freeze the global review, size choice, and final confounder/modifier roles before final estimation. Reuse the exact 800/200 split and saved Gemma 4 26B A4B extractions; do not rerun extraction.
   2. Fit three production seeds with the consolidated roles. Also fit three matched-residual seeds for both the compact and broad modifier sets on the earlier fixed 720/180 population.
   3. Freeze all predictions before opening oracle values. Evaluate direct variable recovery, ITE correlation when defined, RMSE, bias, variability, and population eligibility. Compare with earlier saved selectors on the matched population.
   4. Keep all artifacts in this separate dated directory. Report the broad-selection baseline and consolidation results without selecting variants based on outer-test or oracle performance.
